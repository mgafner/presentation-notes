# Presentation Notes: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Presentation Notes is a plugin for the Even Realities G2 glasses. It runs inside the Even app on your phone and shows your speaker notes on the glasses.

**Developer and contact:** Martin Gafner, presentation-notes@networksfactory.com

## What the app stores

- Your presentations (names and Markdown text), your settings, your position in each presentation and an unsent feedback draft.
- Everything is stored only on your phone: in the storage the Even app provides to plugins and in the local storage of the app's web view.
- None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- When you delete a presentation in the app, it is deleted from this storage.

## Network use (permission "network")

The app uses the network for one thing only: the optional feedback form in the settings. Nothing is sent unless you write a message and tap **Send**.

**What is sent:** the type of message (bug, wish or praise), your message, the email address for a reply if you enter one, the app version and the app language. Your presentations are never sent.

**Where it goes:**

1. To a webhook of Pabbly Connect (`https://connect.pabbly.com`), an automation service used by the developer.
2. Pabbly Connect forwards it by email, through the developer's Google Gmail account, to presentation-notes@networksfactory.com.

Pabbly and Google process the message as service providers of the developer and may store it in their systems (for example in the workflow history or the mailbox). Their servers may be located outside your country, including the United States.

**Why:** to read and answer your feedback and to improve the app.

**How long:** messages are kept as long as needed to handle them. Ask for deletion at any time at presentation-notes@networksfactory.com.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no cookies for tracking.
- No access to camera, microphone, location or photos.
- No sale or sharing of data.

## Your rights

You can ask what feedback messages from you the developer holds, and have them corrected or deleted, by writing to presentation-notes@networksfactory.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same place with a new date.
